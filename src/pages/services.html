Service FAQs.

Start here if you’re unsure what to ask.

Can I enquire about an installation?

Yes. Include the type of fixture or system you’re considering and whether there’s an existing installation in that spot.

What if I don’t know which service to choose?

Select “I’m not sure” on the enquiry form and describe what you’re noticing. A symptom is enough to start.

Can I include more than one issue?

Choose the main issue in the service list and mention the others in the description. Include the affected rooms or fixtures.
